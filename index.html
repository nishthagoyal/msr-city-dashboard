RAG logic explained
RAG status = (Actual Spent + Commitment) ÷ Budget

🔴 Red — deployed >95% of budget (overrun risk)
🟡 Amber — deployed 75–95% of budget (monitor closely)
🟢 Green — deployed <75% of budget (on track)

Recurring detection: REC = appears in 6+ distinct months with avg gap ≤2 months. SEMI = 4–5 distinct months.
